01 / Master

Mastrify Master

  1. Open Applications › Mastrify › Mastrify Master.
  2. Drop your WAV, AIFF, FLAC or MP3 into the app.
  3. Choose your mastering settings.
  4. Process the track.
  5. Listen to the preview.
  6. Pay when you're happy.
  7. Download the full WAV.

02 / Analysis

Mastrify Analysis

  1. Add Mastrify Analysis last on your master bus.In Logic: Audio Units › Mastrify › Mastrify Analysis
  2. Click Analyze my mix.
  3. Press Play in your DAW.
  4. Let the track play.
  5. Run the analysis.
  6. Use the results to improve your mix before mastering.

Troubleshooting

If the plugin doesn't appear

  • Restart your DAW.
  • Open the DAW's plugin manager.
  • Rescan plugins.
  • In Logic, check Audio Units › Mastrify › Mastrify Analysis.

Troubleshooting

If Mastrify Master doesn't start

  • Make sure you're running macOS 12 or later.
  • Make sure you have an internet connection.
  • Quit and reopen Mastrify Master.
